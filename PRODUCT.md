# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential employers and clients evaluating a solo full-stack developer. Users arrive with limited time, seeking rapid proof of technical capability, reliability, and professionalism. They evaluate both the portfolio content (past work, skills) and the portfolio site itself as evidence of craft and attention to detail.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site that showcases full-stack development skills, completed projects, and professional expertise to attract employment and freelance opportunities. The site itself is a working portfolio—both the content and the execution demonstrate capability. Success means employers and clients click through, understand the developer's range and depth, trust the quality of work, and feel confident reaching out.

## Brand Personality

**Trustworthy, reliable, innovative.** Professional and grounded, not flashy. The brand communicates competence through clarity and craft—clean execution, well-organized information, custom thoughtful design that sets it apart from generic portfolio builders. Innovative within the bounds of professionalism: intentional choices in layout, typography, or interaction that show design thinking without sacrificing clarity.

## Anti-references

- Generic portfolio-builder templates (Wix, Squarespace default portfolios, overly polished SaaS UX)
- Overly decorative or experimental design that prioritizes aesthetics over legibility
- Flat, uninspired color palettes or typography hierarchies
- Layouts that hide or bury project details; hierarchy should guide the eye to what matters

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration**: Every design choice serves to communicate skills or guide the viewer through projects. No elements exist just to "look cool."
2. **Custom over template**: Visible craft and intentional choices (not AI-slapped defaults) should signal the developer built this themselves.
3. **Trust through detail**: Attention to spacing, contrast, type hierarchy, responsive behavior, and interaction all signal care and professionalism.
4. **Content-led hierarchy**: Projects and skills are the hero; site infrastructure is invisible scaffolding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA standard compliance required. All interactive elements must be keyboard-navigable, color contrasts must meet 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text. Reduced motion alternatives for any animations. Focus states clearly visible.
